What industry is this work in?

This tool calculates NIOSH-recommended work-rest cycles for indoor workers exposed to heat — in about 30 seconds.

The purpose of this site is to identify the need for breaks from work in hot conditions based on the intensity of the work and the environmental conditions:

  1. Pick your industry — choose the sector that best matches your workplace. ↗ click to see
  2. Describe the work — select the physical activity level (light, moderate, or heavy). ↗ click to see
  3. Acclimatization status — indicate whether the worker is new, returning after time off, or fully acclimatized. This affects the safe exposure limits. ↗ click to see
  4. Measure and enter the heat — use a WBGT meter, a thermometer + humidity reading, or a standard thermometer. The tool recommends work-rest minutes per hour based on what you can measure. ↗ click to see
  • 🌡️ WBGT meters are the gold standard — they measure air temperature, humidity, and radiant heat, and give the most complete picture of heat stress.
  • 💧 Heat index meters (temperature + humidity) are a solid second choice.
  • ⚠️ Thermometers alone are not sufficient. Organizations relying on a plain thermometer during hot conditions should assume 100% relative humidity — this tool does that automatically — to ensure workers are fully protected. Organizations with workers exposed to heat should invest in instruments that can actually measure it. Workers and their representatives have every right to transparent access to those measurements.
  • 🧑‍💼 Supervisors — check conditions before a shift and post the recommended schedule for the crew.
  • 🦺 Safety reps & union stewards — run through scenarios during a training or walkthrough to show workers what protection looks like in practice.
  • 📋 Safety committees — document conditions and recommended cycles as part of a heat illness prevention plan.
