† Heat Index from Temperature + RH — Rothfusz Regression (NOAA, 1990): HI = −42.379 + 2.04901523T + 10.14333127RH − 0.22475541T·RH − 0.00683783T² − 0.05481717RH² + 0.00122874T²·RH + 0.00085282T·RH² − 0.00000199T²·RH² (where T = air temperature in °F, RH = relative humidity in %). Adjustments: when RH < 13% and 80°F < T < 112°F, subtract (13−RH)/4 × √((17−|T−95|)/17); when RH > 85% and 80°F < T < 87°F, add ((RH−85)/10) × ((87−T)/5). When T < 80°F or the simple estimate < 80°F, the Steadman (1979) approximation applies: HI = 0.5 × [T + 61.0 + (T−68.0)×1.2 + RH×0.094]. Source: Rothfusz, L.P. (1990). The heat index equation. NWS Technical Attachment SR 90-23. Steadman, R.G. (1979). The assessment of sultriness. Part I: A temperature-humidity index based on human physiology and clothing science. Journal of Applied Meteorology, 18(7), 861–873.
