Temperature + Relative Humidity → Work-Rest Recommendation

Find dry-bulb temperature in the left column and relative humidity across the top. Each cell shows the approximate Heat Index and the recommended work-rest cycle for the selected worker status and activity level. Heat index is derived from air temperature and RH; WBGT is a more complete measure for high-radiant environments. Indoor, no solar load. Standard work clothing assumed. Note: NIOSH Recommended Alert Limits (RAL, for unacclimatized workers) and Recommended Exposure Limits (REL, for acclimatized workers) are expressed in WBGT units, not Heat Index. Unacclimatized thresholds in this tab are methodology-derived approximations — no NIOSH-published equivalent exists in HI units.

How to use: Set worker status at the top of the page, select the activity level, then find the row for current air temperature and column for relative humidity. Colors and cycles update automatically. CONT* = continuous work is acceptable but active monitoring, buddy system, and ready access to water are required.
Limitations: Heat Index uses air temperature and relative humidity only — it does not account for air movement, radiant heat from hot equipment or surfaces, clothing, or metabolic rate. In high-radiant environments (kitchens, laundries, foundries, bakeries), measure WBGT directly. Heat index values shown are approximate; verify with NOAA tables or the Rothfusz regression equation.
