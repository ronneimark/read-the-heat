Imminent danger. All work should cease until conditions are corrected. Evacuation if engineering controls cannot reduce heat to safe levels.
Hydration guidance (consistent with OSHA/NIOSH): Workers should drink approximately 1 cup (8 oz / 240 mL) of cool water every 15–20 minutes — not waiting until thirsty. Avoid caffeine and alcohol. Electrolyte replacement is important for prolonged exposures (>2 hours) with heavy sweating.
Key limitation: Heat index was developed for shade conditions with 5 mph wind. Direct radiant heat from equipment, ovens, or hot surfaces; very high or low air movement; or PPE/impermeable clothing — heat index significantly underestimates actual heat stress. WBGT is preferred in these settings.
