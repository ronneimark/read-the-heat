Has this worker completed heat acclimatization?

✅
Acclimatized
Has completed heat acclimatization and has not been absent from heat work for a week or more. Work-rest cycles use the NIOSH Recommended Exposure Limits (REL).
  • Experienced worker in continuous heat exposure
  • Returning worker absent fewer than 7 days
  • Acclimatization increases sweating efficiency, stabilizes circulation, and lowers core temperature and heart rate at the same workload (NIOSH, 2016)
⚠️
Unacclimatized — New Worker
Has not previously worked in heat or has not yet completed an acclimatization ramp. Uses the more protective NIOSH Recommended Alert Limits (RAL).
  • New employee starting heat work for the first time
  • Worker newly assigned to a hot area
  • NIOSH protocol: ≤20% of usual exposure on Day 1, +20% each day over 7–14 days
  • Per NIOSH (2016): "lack of acclimatization has been shown to be a major factor associated with worker heat-related illness and death"
🔄
Unacclimatized — Returning Worker
Previously acclimatized, but absent from heat work for 7 or more days. Uses the more protective NIOSH Recommended Alert Limits (RAL).
  • Worker returning after 7+ days away from heat exposure
  • Workers with cardiovascular disease, obesity, certain medications, or prior heat illness
  • NIOSH return protocol: 4-day ramp — 50% → 60% → 80% → 100% of usual exposure
