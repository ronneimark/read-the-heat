What type of clothing or PPE is the worker wearing?

👕
Standard Work Clothing
Typical work clothes that allow sweat to evaporate normally. No adjustment is applied to WBGT thresholds — NIOSH limits assume standard summer work clothing.
  • Cotton or poly-blend shirts and pants
  • Standard work coveralls (single-layer, breathable)
  • Hard hat, gloves, safety glasses, steel-toed boots
  • Most standard hi-vis vests
No adjustment — standard assumption
🦺
Impermeable or Vapor-Barrier PPE
PPE that blocks sweat evaporation, substantially increasing heat strain at any given temperature. A +10°C correction is applied to the measured WBGT, lowering all effective exposure thresholds (NIOSH, 2016; ACGIH, 2025, Table 2).
  • Chemical-resistant or hazmat suits
  • Impermeable rain gear or rubber/PVC suits
  • Limited-use vapor-barrier coveralls (e.g., Tyvek)
  • Fully encapsulating protective ensembles
+10°C (+18°F) correction applied to WBGT
